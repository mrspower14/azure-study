1. .env 파일 만들고 프로젝트 엔드포인트와 에이전트 이름 업데이트

2. 필요한 패키지 설치
npm install @azure/ai-projects @azure/identity dotenv

3. 애플리케이션을 실행.
node .\friday-agent-func.js

4. 다음의 프롬프트를 입력해 함수를 호출해서 응답하는지 테스트
- 프롬프트1: Find me the next event I can see from South America and give me the cost for 5 hours of premium telescope time at normal priority.

(예상 응답)
 AGENT: The next astronomical event you can observe from South America is the Jupiter-Venus Conjunction, taking place on May 1st.
 The cost for 5 hours of premium telescope time at normal priority for this observation will be $1,875. 

- 프롬프트2:  Generate that information in a report for Bellows College.

(예상 응답)
 AGENT: Here is your report for Bellows College:

 - Next visible astronomical event: Jupiter-Venus Conjunction
 - Date: May 1st
 - Visible from: South America
 - Observation details:
     - Telescope tier: Premium
     - Duration: 5 hours
     - Priority: Normal
 - Observation cost: $1,875

 A formal report has been generated for Bellows College.

- 생성된 리포트 확인

5. Foundry Toolkit에서 생성된 에이전트 astronomy-agent 확인

6. quit 입력해 프로그램 종료하고 에이전트 삭제 확인
